# Earjar

Earjar is an audio software and hardware startup building VSTs, plugins, synthesizers, libraries and other gear for anyone who wants to make music.
Our stuff is a little strange, a little playful, and built to sound genuinely beautiful.

<br>

## What we make

`VSTs` `plugins` `synthesizers` `libraries`

- **VSTs & plugins**, software instruments and effects
- **Synthesizers**, hardware, unconventional by design
- **Libraries**, libraries that other developers can use in their projects

Our hardware line is in development. Synths and tuners that don't look like anything else out there, but sound like they belong on your next record.
